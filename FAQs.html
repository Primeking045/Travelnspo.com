How does a Travel Loan actually work?›

We book your flight or bus ticket for you upfront, and you repay it in installments — pay in full, or split into 2, 3, or 4 payments. It's your trip financed, not a cash loan.

What do I need to apply?›

Your NIN or BVN, and a quick face verification to confirm it's you. Approval is usually instant once both checks pass.

What happens if I miss a payment?›

You'll get a reminder before it's due. Missed payments may affect your ability to take future Travel Loans, so we'll always notify you ahead of time.

Why is my fare shown as a range, not a fixed price?›

Fares shift with fuel prices and demand, so we show an honest range based on recent trips instead of a number we can't guarantee. Your driver confirms the exact price before you ride.

Can I use my ride credit on any ride type?›

Yes — credit earned in Rewards can be applied to Okada, Keke, or Car rides through Driver Connect.

How do I change my PIN?›

Go to Settings → Change PIN. You'll confirm your current PIN, then set and confirm a new one.

Is my NIN/BVN stored on your servers?›

No — we only keep a verified/true-false flag and the last 4 digits for display. The actual verification happens through a licensed KYC provider.

What is auto-save on a New Plan?›

When creating a travel plan, you can turn on auto-save to move money automatically from a linked bank account on a weekly or monthly schedule until you hit your target.

Can I withdraw my savings anytime?›

Yes, your savings balance is always yours — withdraw or apply it toward any trip whenever you like.
